On 4 November 2026 I was a panellist on "AI and Digital Media: Beyond the Algorithms", a roundtable hosted by the Department of Film Studies at the English and Foreign Languages University (EFLU), Hyderabad. I opened with a short talk and then joined the discussion and questions.

## The panel

- Dr. Meraj Mubarki, Maulana Azad National Urdu University
- Rima Mazumdar, Head of Editing, Annapurna College
- Srikanth Manchem, Seeneemaa
- Glen D'Silva, EFLU
- Matt Bennett, University of Cincinnati Blue Ash College
